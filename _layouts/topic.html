--- layout: default --- {% comment %} Topic archive page. Top to bottom: 1. Section header - topic name, optional subhead, right-hand image that fades into the background 2. Featured story - optional; stats call-out OR three articles beside it 3. Latest research - the next 4 articles, as cards (one row on desktop) 4. Video / spotlight - the topic's newest video; if a faculty member is spotlighted, they take 2/3 width and the video 1/3 5. More in - the remaining articles as a list, first batch shown, "View more research" reveals the rest 6. "Connect with our experts" call-out Everything configurable lives in _data/topic_pages.yml (CMS: Site Settings > Topic pages); see the notes at the top of that file for the defaults used when a field is left blank. Articles are handed out in order, newest first, and never repeat: whatever the featured story and its sidebar use is left out of the cards, and whatever the cards use is left out of the list. Includes (card.html, article-row.html, the badges, media-callout.html) assign variables of their own, and in Liquid an assign inside an include is visible to this page afterwards.
