Global rankings of accounting research get compiled every year by Brigham Young University, using publication counts across the field's top journals to rank programs worldwide. For 2025, KU's accounting faculty held onto the No. 1 spot in audit archival research — the empirical, data-driven end of audit scholarship — and placed No. 2 globally across auditing publications overall.

Rankings like these are a lagging indicator of a research culture built over years, not a single breakthrough paper. Maintaining a No. 1 position year over year means a sustained pipeline of faculty publishing in the field's most selective journals, not a one-time outlier result.

## Why this ranking specifically matters

Audit archival research — studies that mine real audit and financial-reporting data rather than lab experiments or surveys — is one of the more resource- and access-intensive corners of accounting scholarship. A sustained top ranking here signals both faculty depth and the kind of institutional relationships with data sources that take a long time to build.
