A top ranking once can be a fluke of timing. A top ranking five years running is a track record.

For the fifth year in a row, accounting faculty at the University of Kansas School of Business earned a No. 1 global ranking for their audit archival research contributions over the past six and 12 years, according to Brigham Young University's 2023 rankings — one of the field's most closely watched annual measures of research output at top journals.

## What sustains a streak like this

Audit archival research — empirical, data-driven scholarship built on real audit and financial-reporting data — is a resource-intensive corner of accounting research, both in the access it requires and the faculty depth it takes to sustain over multiple years. A single strong publication year can happen almost anywhere; five years running reflects an established pipeline rather than a one-time result.

The ranking measures publications across the discipline's most selective journals, which means each year's result depends on faculty work that, in many cases, started years earlier — a reminder that rankings like these are a lagging indicator of investment in a research culture, not a snapshot of any single semester.
