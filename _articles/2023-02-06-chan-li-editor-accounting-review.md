Editorial appointments at the field's top journals are a quiet but meaningful measure of a program's standing. KU accounting has one of its own serving a second term at the very top of that list.

Chan Li, C.A. Scupin Professor at the University of Kansas School of Business, will serve a second term as an editor for The Accounting Review, widely regarded as the premier journal for accounting research.

## What the role involves

Journal editors at this level oversee the peer-review process for submitted research, shaping which studies meet the bar for publication in one of the field's most selective outlets — work that happens largely out of public view, but carries real influence over what accounting research gets published and how the field's conversation develops.

A reappointment, rather than a first-time selection, signals the journal's confidence in Li's judgment over a full term already served — a form of recognition that compounds rather than resets with each cycle.
