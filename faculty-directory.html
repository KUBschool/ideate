--- layout: default title: "Faculty" permalink: /faculty/ description: "Browse KU School of Business faculty and their research featured on Ideate." --- {% comment %} Faculty directory: every faculty member as a card, A-Z by LAST name, with filters for academic area and research topic (and a name search) that app.js switches on. Last name = the "Last name" field on the faculty profile when it is filled in; otherwise the final word of the full name, plus any lowercase words just before it ("den Nieuwenboer", "van der Berg"). Everyone is sorted on that, then on the full name. A person's research topics are the topics of the Ideate stories they are tagged in (a story's main topic and its "Also relates to" topics, worked out by topic-links.html). Written for GitHub Pages (Jekyll 3.10): no "and" / "or" inside a where_exp filter.
